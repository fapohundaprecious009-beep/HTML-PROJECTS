FAQ

Answers to popular questions

How long does the installation of doors/windows take?

Installation time depends on the number of doors or windows and the complexity of the project. Most standard installations can be completed within 1–3 days, while larger projects may take longer.

What color options are available for doors/windows?

We offer a variety of colors and finishes, including white, black, grey, brown, and wood finishes.

Are there any special discounts or promotions currently available?

We occasionally offer special discounts and promotions on selected doors, windows, and installation services.

What are the delivery times for the selected doors/windows?

Delivery times depend on the product selected, availability, and customization requirements.

Can you provide a free consultation for product selection?

Yes! We offer free consultations to help you choose the right doors and windows based on your space, design preferences, budget, and requirements.
