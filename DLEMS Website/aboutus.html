More Than A School,
We Build Futures.

De-Leaders Empire Model Schools is committed to providing a nurturing, stimulating and supportive learning environment where every child can thrive.

Our goal is not only to prepare children for examinations, but also to prepare them for life.

——— WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Core Values

These values guide how we teach, lead and nurture every child entrusted to us.

🎓

QUALITY TEACHING

We are committed to delivering quality teaching that helps every learner understand, grow and achieve their full potential.

💼

PROFESSIONALISM

We maintain high professional standards in our teaching, conduct and responsibilities towards our learners and the school community.

🛡️

INTEGRITY

We promote honesty, transparency and strong moral principles in everything we do.

🤝

TEAM WORK

We believe in working together, supporting one another and combining our strengths to achieve better results.

HARD WORK
